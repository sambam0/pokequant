Fill this in and both operators sign before mixing capital, inventory, or software into the shared pilot.

## Decision rights

- Domain owner approves condition, authenticity, and buy/skip.
- Technology owner may veto for missing data, exposure breach, or negative economics.
- Both approve exceptions above joint-approval threshold, authenticity uncertainty, and out-of-scope purchases.
